A grid table draws cell borders with `+` and `-`.

+-------------+--------------+
| Term        | Meaning      |
+=============+==============+
| Grid table  | Cells are    |
|             | drawn with + |
+-------------+--------------+

A grid table can carry a footer, marked by an extra `=` separator:

+---------+-------+
| Item    | Price |
+=========+=======+
| Eggs    | 5     |
+---------+-------+
| Milk    | 3     |
+=========+=======+
| Total   | 8     |
+=========+=======+
