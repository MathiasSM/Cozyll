---
title: "Forms"
date: 2024-06-02
description: "Form controls: inputs, selects, textareas, and buttons."
path: /elements/forms
---

Form controls are styled by the generator; whether a site uses them is up to its content.

```{=html}
<form action="#">
  <fieldset>
    <legend>An example form</legend>
    <p><label>Name <input type="text" placeholder="Jane Doe" /></label></p>
    <p><label>Role <select><option>Developer</option><option>Designer</option><option>Writer</option></select></label></p>
    <p><label>Message <textarea rows="3" placeholder="Say something…"></textarea></label></p>
    <p><button type="button">Send</button></p>
  </fieldset>
</form>
```
